Enrique de la Hoz

Associate Professor. Computer Science Department, University of Alcalá.

Escuela Politécnica Superior

Ctra. Madrid-Barcelona, km 33,600

28805 Alcalá de Henares, Spain

I am an associate professor at the University of Alcalá (Spain), in the Telematics Engineering area, and a member of the NetIS (Networks and Intelligent Systems) research group.

My research covers network and systems security, wireless network optimization through graph coloring, critical infrastructure resilience, and automated negotiation in multi-agent systems, the topic of my PhD thesis (2017).

I teach networking and security courses in the Telecommunication and Computer Engineering degrees and in the Master's in Cybersecurity. Some of my interactive teaching resources are available in English:
